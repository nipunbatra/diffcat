pages aligned, not just numbered

An inserted cover page or a re-flowed chapter doesn't make every later page look "changed": pages are matched by their content, and pages that were merged or split are shown with their counterpart.

a text diff that survives typesetting

Running heads, page numbers, hyphenation and font changes are ignored; the text of both files is diffed as one document, word by word, with relocated passages shown as moves rather than edits.

local by design

A strict Content-Security-Policy forbids all network requests, so the page couldn't upload your files even if it wanted to. Load it once and it works offline. Open source, AGPL-3.0.
